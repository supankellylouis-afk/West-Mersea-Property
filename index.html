The Property

Where the estuary
meets unhurried luxury

Nestled on the tranquil island of West Mersea — accessible by the ancient Strood causeway — 2 City Road is a beautifully appointed four-bedroom, 160m² holiday home offering front-row seats to one of England's most atmospheric coastlines. The entire place is yours for the duration of your stay — no shared spaces, no compromises.

Step outside to the scent of salt air, the sound of the tide, and the slow theatre of sailing boats. Unwind in the private hot tub as the sun sets over the estuary, or fire up the BBQ on the terrace for a relaxed evening with family. Inside, considered design and premium furnishings create a home that is warm, restful, and quietly spectacular.

From the well-equipped kitchen where you'll prepare fresh catch from the local oyster sheds, to spacious family rooms and private bathrooms — every detail has been curated for a stay you will not wish to leave. Free on-site parking, free high-speed Wi-Fi (482 Mbps), and a warm welcome for well-behaved pets complete the picture.

Find Us

West Mersea,
Essex

West Mersea sits on Mersea Island — England's most easterly inhabited island — connected to the mainland by the tidal Strood causeway. It is approximately 8 miles south of Colchester and reachable in around 90 minutes from central London.

  • Colchester — 8 miles (20 min drive)
  • Mersea Island Beach — 5 min walk
  • The Company Shed — 5 min walk
  • London Liverpool St — 90 min by train & taxi
  • Stansted Airport — 40 min drive
