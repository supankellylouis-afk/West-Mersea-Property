The Property

Where the estuary
meets unhurried luxury

Nestled on the tranquil island of West Mersea — accessible by the ancient Strood causeway — 2 City Road is a beautifully appointed four-bedroom holiday home offering front-row seats to one of England's most atmospheric coastlines.

Step outside to the scent of salt air, the sound of the tide, and the slow theatre of sailing boats. Inside, considered design and premium furnishings create a home that is warm, restful, and quietly spectacular.

From the well-equipped kitchen where you'll prepare fresh catch from the local oyster sheds, to the sunlit terrace perfect for evening sundowners — every detail has been curated for a stay you will not wish to leave.

4 Bedrooms

High-Speed Wi-Fi

Fibre broadband throughout the property — ideal for remote working or streaming your favourite series.

Private Garden & Terrace

Landscaped south-facing garden with outdoor dining set, BBQ, and views across the Blackwater Estuary.

Sea Views

Panoramic estuary views from the living room and master bedroom — watch the tide and the sailing boats drift past.

West Mersea is world-famous for its oysters. The Company Shed and other fish sheds are a five-minute walk away.

Coastal Activities

Kayaking, sailing, cycling and beach walks — all on your doorstep. Boards and bikes available for hire nearby.

Premium Linens & Toiletries

Find Us

West Mersea,
Essex

West Mersea sits on Mersea Island — England's most easterly inhabited island — connected to the mainland by the tidal Strood causeway. It is approximately 8 miles south of Colchester and reachable in around 90 minutes from central London.

  • Colchester — 8 miles (20 min drive)
  • Mersea Island Beach — 5 min walk
  • The Company Shed — 5 min walk
  • London Liverpool St — 90 min by train & taxi
  • Stansted Airport — 40 min drive
